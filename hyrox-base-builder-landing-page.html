Why Most Free Plans Fall Short

Most free HYROX plans are a running plan with some workouts bolted on, or a station-work list with no running structure behind it. Neither reflects race day: eight stations, each one preceded by a kilometre of running on legs that are already compromised from the station before.

This program is built around one principle: train the transition, not just the pieces. Every week includes sessions where you run into fatigue and then hit a station, or hit a station and then run — because that's the actual demand of the race.

How It's Structured

3 sessions a week, 60-75 minutes each, over 8 weeks:

WEEKS 1-3

Base

Work capacity across all 8 stations, running base, technique under mild fatigue.

WEEKS 4-6

Build

Increased loads and intensity, stations combined back-to-back, compromised running.

WEEKS 7-8

Peak

Partial then full race simulation, taper into your test — or your actual race.

Who It's For

You've got a running base (comfortable covering 5-8km) and you already know your way around the eight stations — whether from racing before, training on your own, or working through the HYROX beginner guide. This is the bridge from "capable" to "race ready," not a couch-to-HYROX plan.
